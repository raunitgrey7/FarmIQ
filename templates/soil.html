Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this soil test?

Our method follows USDA guidelines and achieves 85-95% accuracy compared to lab tests when performed correctly with proper soil moisture.

When is the best time to test soil?

Test soil when it's moist but not wet, typically 24-48 hours after rainfall or irrigation. Avoid testing when soil is bone dry or waterlogged.

Can I test different areas separately?

Yes! Soil can vary across your property. Test multiple locations and note the differences for precise management.

How often should I test my soil?

For optimal results, test your soil at least once per season or whenever you notice significant changes in plant growth.
